# Code of Conduct

Terminal Email is a shared, public resource. Keep every issue, pull request and discussion useful and respectful.

- Argue with sources, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and say when you maintain or work on a project being discussed.
- No harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, spam or self-promotion.
- Do not post anyone's private information, including your own.

Maintainers may edit, hide or lock comments and block accounts that break these rules. Report problems by opening an issue or emailing support@forwardemail.net.
